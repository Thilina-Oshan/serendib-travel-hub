Things you can only do here

Ceylon tea tasting

Walk the estates around Nuwara Eliya, watch leaves rolled and fired, then taste the result at the source.

🐆

Yala safari

Jeep through dry-zone scrub at dawn. Yala has one of the world's densest leopard populations.

🏄

Surfing in Arugam Bay

A long right-hand point break on the east coast, best from May to September.

🚂

Scenic train rides

Kandy to Ella is among the world's great rail journeys. Book reserved seats early.
